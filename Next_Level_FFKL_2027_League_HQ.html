SECTION 1: LEAGUE DUES

League dues must be paid in full through LeagueSafe no later than July 30, 2027.

League dues will increase by $5 per team each season until reaching $150. Any increase beyond $150 must be approved by league vote.
  • No offseason trades will be approved until all owners involved have paid their league dues in full.
  • Owners who fail to pay by the deadline may be subject to late fees determined by the Commissioner.
  • Owners who have not paid their dues may lose league privileges, including participation in the draft, until payment has been received.

SECTION 2: OWNER RESPONSIBILITIES & TEAM ABANDONMENT

Rule 2.1 – Active Team Management

Every owner is expected to actively manage their team throughout the entire season, including the playoffs.

  • Submit a complete and competitive lineup every week.
  • Replace inactive, suspended, injured, or bye-week players whenever possible.
  • Respond to Commissioner communications regarding league matters.

Rule 2.2 – Team Abandonment

Owners who repeatedly fail to set a lineup or manage their team without communicating with the Commissioner may be considered to have abandoned their franchise.

  • The Commissioner may place the team under commissioner control.
  • The team may remain on auto control until a replacement owner is found.
  • The owner forfeits all league dues.
  • Any unpaid league winnings are forfeited.

A second offense within the same season, including the playoffs, will result in removal from the league.

Rule 2.3 – Cheating & Collusion

Any owner suspected of cheating or collusion may be investigated by the Commissioner.

  • Dropping elite players to benefit another team.
  • Intentionally making unfair or one-sided trades.
  • Purposely setting incomplete or noncompetitive lineups.
  • Tanking games to manipulate playoff standings.
  • Circumventing keeper or draft rules.
  • Any action intended to compromise league integrity.

Penalties may include trade reversal, loss of draft picks, removal of keepers, suspension, or permanent removal from the league. The Commissioner’s ruling is final unless a league vote is requested.

SECTION 3: LEAGUE VOTING

League votes (excluding trades) will be conducted through the ESPN Fantasy League Manager Poll. All owners are expected to participate before the stated deadline.

Failure to vote may result in the owner losing voting privileges for future league matters. Unless otherwise stated, league proposals require unanimous approval to pass.

SECTION 4: TRADES

Trades will be reviewed through ESPN Fantasy Football. League members may vote to approve or veto trades.

The Commissioner reserves the right to overturn or reject any trade involving collusion, league manipulation, circumvention of league rules, or clear competitive imbalance intended to damage league integrity.

The Commissioner’s decision is final.

SECTION 5: ROUNDED KEEPER RULES

The league uses the Rounded Keeper Format. Owners may keep eligible players from their previous season’s final roster before the annual draft.

Rule 5.1 – Keeper Cost

A kept player costs one draft round earlier than where they were originally drafted the previous season.

Example: Drafted in Round 8 → kept the following season for a Round 7 pick.

Each additional season a player is kept moves their keeper cost two rounds earlier until reaching Round 1.

First-round players may not be kept by the team that originally drafted them. However, they may be traded for draft picks or draft picks plus players, and the acquiring team may keep that player at first-round value.

Rule 5.2 – Keeper Deadline

July 30 at 5:00 PM Eastern Time

All keepers must be selected through the ESPN Fantasy App. The Commissioner will NOT reopen keeper selections after the deadline. Owners are solely responsible for selecting their own keepers.

Rule 5.3 – Commissioner Responsibilities

The Commissioner is responsible for verifying and recording each keeper’s draft-round value before the draft. Previous draft history is available through the ESPN Fantasy Draft Recap (web version only).

Rule 5.4 – Free Agents

Players who were undrafted during the previous season but remain on an owner’s final roster may be kept. Undrafted players automatically carry an 8th-round keeper value.

Rule 5.5 – Keeper Value & Player Movement

A player’s keeper value is determined by the round in which they were originally drafted and follows the player for as long as they remain keeper eligible.

If a team has two keepers that require the same value to be kept, the keeper that’s been on the team the longest will need to be kept with one round higher.

If a player changes teams through a trade or waiver claim, their original draft round and current keeper value remain unchanged.

Rule 5.6 – Traded Draft Picks - Keepers

A kept player must be selected using the draft pick that matches their current keeper value. If that pick was traded away or used for a keeper, the keeper moves to the next earlier available pick.

Example 1: A 5th-round keeper moves to the 4th if the 5th is unavailable.
Example 2: If the 5th and 4th are unavailable, the keeper moves to the 3rd.

Owners may not use a later draft pick to keep a player whose required keeper round has been traded away.

Rule 5.7 – Offseason Trades

All offseason trades involving players or draft picks are subject to Commissioner review. Both owners must have paid their league dues in full through LeagueSafe before any offseason trade will be processed.

COMMISSIONER’S AUTHORITY

The Commissioner is responsible for enforcing league rules, protecting competitive integrity, and resolving disputes not specifically addressed within this Constitution.

Any situation not covered by these rules will be decided by the Commissioner in the best interest of the league.

The Commissioner’s decision shall be considered final unless otherwise stated by league vote.
